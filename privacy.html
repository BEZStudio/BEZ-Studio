Privacy Notice

What BEZ Studio collects and why.

Data-minimization choice: your date of birth is used during signup to calculate whether you are at least 13, then the raw birth date is discarded rather than stored in the account database.

Information we collect

Firebase Authentication can store your display name, email address, email-verification state and account metadata. BEZ Studio also uses local browser storage for your download library in this static edition.

Email verification

BEZ Studio sends a verification email link to the email address entered during signup. Firebase handles verification securely without BEZ Studio storing email-verification codes.

Why information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, enforce the 13+ requirement, deliver account verification messages, provide account-gated game downloads, show library history and operate the service.

Cookies

Firebase Authentication may use browser storage to keep users signed in. BEZ Studio does not use advertising trackers in this release.

Retention

Firebase account data remains according to your Firebase project settings. Download-library history in this edition is stored locally in the browser and can be cleared by the user.

Young users

BEZ Studio does not allow account creation by users under 13. Additional parent or guardian consent requirements may apply in some places for users who are minors.

Security

Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication. Private secrets must never be placed in public GitHub Pages files. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so the platform should be kept updated and served over HTTPS before public deployment.

Contact

Privacy questions and account-data requests can be submitted through the official Contact page.
